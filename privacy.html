Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Passaround is built to work entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means for your information.

Information we collect

None. Passaround does not collect, store, or send any personal information to the developer or to anyone else. The app has no account and does not use the internet.

Camera and microphone

Passaround asks for the camera and microphone so people can record their video messages. Recordings are saved only inside the app on your iPhone. They are never uploaded.

Information saved on your device

  • Your cards: titles, closing lines, colors and settings
  • The video messages people record, and the names they type

The developer cannot see any of this. Deleting a card deletes its videos, and deleting the app removes everything.

Photos

When you tap Add a video someone sent you, the iOS picker shares only the one video you choose. When you tap Save to Photos, Passaround asks for permission to add the finished video to your library. It does not read your library.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the finished video is handed to the iOS share sheet and you choose where it goes. The developer does not receive it.

Analytics, ads, and tracking

Passaround contains no analytics tools, no advertising, no third-party code, and no tracking of any kind.

Children

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
