Passaround

A group video card made on one phone. Pass it around, everyone records a short message, and you get one video with everyone's names on screen.

How it works

1. Make a card

Pick who it's for and the occasion: birthday, graduation, thank you, farewell and more. Choose colors and how long each message can be.

2. Pass the phone

Each person taps Record my message, types their name and records with the front camera. Messages stay a surprise until the end.

3. Make the video

Passaround joins every message into one video with an opening title, each person's name on screen and a closing card. Share it or save it to Photos.

Questions

Someone couldn't be there. Can they still join?

Yes. Ask them to record a video on their own phone and send it to you. Then tap Add a video someone sent you and type their name.

Do I need an account or internet?

No. Everything happens on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded. The only time the app goes online is if you buy or restore Passaround Pro.

Can I change the order or fix a name?

Yes. On the card, tap the ... menu, then Edit to fix names or turn off the surprise setting. Tap Edit in the same menu to reorder or delete messages.

The camera doesn't open.

Open Settings, then Passaround, and turn on Camera and Microphone.

How long can the video be?

Each recording can be 10 to 20 seconds on the free version, or up to 60 seconds with Pro (you choose). A free card holds 6 messages, and a Pro card holds up to 50. Videos people send you are cut to their first minute.

What does Passaround Pro add?

Passaround is free to use. Passaround Pro is a one-time purchase that adds unlimited messages per card, all 8 color themes, messages up to 60 seconds, and removes the small "Made with Passaround" line from the end of your videos. There is no subscription.

How do I get Pro back on a new phone?

Open Passaround, tap the crown at the top of the home screen, and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with. Family Sharing is supported.
